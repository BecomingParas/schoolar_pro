# BidyaGo – Platform Understanding & Existing Content Update

## PART 1: Understand the BidyaGo Platform

Before making any changes, understand what BidyaGo is, who it serves, how it works, and how it generates revenue.

### 1. What is BidyaGo?

BidyaGo is a scholarship discovery, education opportunity, and application management platform initially focused on helping students from Nepal discover international education opportunities.
 
Our goal is to connect three main groups:

1. Students looking for international scholarships and educational opportunities.
2. Education consultancies that help students with counselling and application processes.
3. Colleges and universities that want to showcase their institutions, programmes, scholarships, and connect with prospective students.

BidyaGo is not simply a scholarship listing website. It is an interconnected education platform that connects students, educational institutions, scholarships, and consultancy services.

Our primary initial market is Nepal, with international scholarship opportunities including Japan, Australia, South Korea, the UK, and Erasmus Mundus programmes.

### 2. How BidyaGo Works

BidyaGo has three main user experiences.

#### A. For Students

Students are the primary audience and users of the platform.

They can:

- Discover international scholarships.
- Explore scholarships by country, academic level, subject, funding type, and deadline.
- View scholarship eligibility requirements.
- Understand tuition coverage, stipends, accommodation, travel support, and other benefits.
- Create a personal academic profile.
- Receive scholarship recommendations based on their profile.
- Save and compare scholarships.
- View required documents and application instructions.
- Track their application progress.
- Manage application tasks and deadlines.
- Receive notifications and reminders.
- Discover education consultancies.
- Explore universities, colleges, and their academic programmes.
- Send enquiries to participating universities or consultancies.
- Choose what personal information and documents they want to share.

**Important student access policy:**

The core scholarship discovery experience should remain FREE.

Students should not have to pay just to:
- Discover scholarships.
- Read scholarship requirements.
- View official application links.
- Access basic scholarship information.
- Understand application procedures.

Optional premium features may be introduced later, such as advanced scholarship matching, unlimited comparisons, advanced application planning, and additional productivity tools.

Possible future pricing:
- Free: Rs. 0
- Plus: Rs. 199/month
- Pro: Rs. 499/month

These prices are proposals and should not be presented as confirmed plans unless the product actually implements them.

#### B. For Education Consultancies

Consultancies are an important paying customer group.

BidyaGo provides consultancies with a business management workspace to organize their student counselling and application operations.

A consultancy can:

- Register its organization and submit verification information.
- Receive approval from BidyaGo administrators.
- Create and manage its consultancy profile.
- Invite employees and counsellors.
- Manage student records and cases.
- Assign students to counsellors.
- Track individual student applications.
- Request and organize student documents.
- Manage application checklists.
- Create tasks and follow-up reminders.
- Schedule appointments.
- Add counselling notes.
- Monitor application deadlines.
- View reports and operational analytics.
- Communicate with students through the platform.

**Why do consultancies pay?**

Many consultancies manage student information through spreadsheets, WhatsApp, emails, and manual follow-ups.

BidyaGo helps them centralize their operations, reduce administrative work, manage more students efficiently, improve counsellor coordination, and provide a more organized student experience.

Consultancies pay for the management software, workflow automation, team collaboration, and business tools—not for unrestricted access to student information.

**Proposed consultancy subscription plans:**

- Starter: Rs. 2,999/month
  - Up to 3 staff members.
  - Up to 30 active student cases.
  - Student management.
  - Tasks and reminders.

- Growth: Rs. 6,999/month
  - Up to 10 staff members.
  - Up to 100 active student cases.
  - Counsellor assignments.
  - Document management.
  - Reports and analytics.

- Pro: Rs. 12,999/month
  - Up to 25 staff members.
  - Up to 300 active student cases.
  - Multiple branches.
  - Advanced reporting.
  - Custom workflows.

These are proposed prices. Do not display them as active subscriptions unless implemented and approved.

#### C. For Universities and Colleges

Universities and colleges are another potential paying customer group.

BidyaGo gives educational institutions an opportunity to reach prospective international students, particularly students from Nepal.

An institution can:

- Register its institution.
- Submit institutional verification documents.
- Receive approval from BidyaGo administrators.
- Create an official institutional profile.
- Publish available academic programmes.
- Display bachelor's, master's, and doctoral programmes.
- Publish admission requirements.
- Display tuition fees and other costs.
- Publish institution-funded scholarships.
- Provide application deadlines and instructions.
- Receive student enquiries.
- Manage participating student applications when an official agreement allows it.
- View student enquiry and recruitment analytics.

**How students connect with universities:**

1. A university publishes its institutional profile and academic programmes.
2. A student discovers the university through BidyaGo.
3. The student views programme details, requirements, tuition, and available scholarships.
4. The student selects "Request Information" or an available application option.
5. The student explicitly chooses which personal information to share.
6. The university receives the enquiry through its dashboard.
7. The university contacts the student and explains the next steps.

**Why do universities pay?**

Universities may pay for increased visibility, promotional opportunities, student enquiry management, recruitment analytics, and integrations with their admission processes.

Possible institution plans:

- Basic: Free institutional profile and basic listings.
- Featured: Paid promotional visibility and enhanced institutional profile.
- Partner: Custom institutional partnerships, enquiry management, application integrations, and analytics.

Featured or sponsored listings must always be clearly labelled. Paid placement must never influence scholarship eligibility matching or the verification status of scholarship information.

### 3. How Scholarship Applications Actually Work

BidyaGo must support different application routes depending on the scholarship provider.

There are two possible workflows.

**Method 1: Apply through the official external source (default)**

1. Student discovers a scholarship on BidyaGo.
2. Student reviews eligibility, benefits, documents, and deadlines.
3. Student clicks "Apply on Official Website".
4. Student completes the application through the official embassy, government, university, or scholarship provider.
5. Student can use BidyaGo to track their application and manage preparation tasks.

**Method 2: Apply or submit an enquiry through BidyaGo (only when supported)**

Some participating universities or institutions may agree to receive enquiries or applications directly through BidyaGo.

In these cases:
1. Student selects the participating institution's application or enquiry option.
2. Student provides the requested information.
3. Student explicitly consents to sharing the submitted information.
4. The institution receives the submission through its dashboard.
5. The institution manages the next steps.

Never imply that every scholarship can be applied for directly through BidyaGo.

Clearly distinguish between:
- Official external application.
- University enquiry.
- Application submitted through an authorized institutional partnership.

BidyaGo does not award scholarships, guarantee admission, or guarantee scholarship selection.

### 4. How BidyaGo Generates Revenue

BidyaGo has three potential revenue sources, but each pays for a different value proposition.

| Customer | What they pay for | Revenue model |
|---|---|---|
| Students | Advanced productivity and planning tools | Optional subscription |
| Consultancies | Student and business management software | Monthly subscription |
| Universities | Institutional visibility, recruitment tools, and integrations | Featured plans and partnerships |

**Our recommended launch strategy:**

1. Keep core student scholarship discovery free to attract a large student audience.
2. Launch consultancy management subscriptions as the first major revenue opportunity.
3. Build student traffic and establish credibility.
4. Approach universities with evidence of student interest and enquiry volume.
5. Introduce optional student premium features after validating actual demand.

The platform should not depend entirely on students paying for information that is already publicly available.

### 5. Important Platform Principles

The following principles must be reflected throughout the website:

- Scholarship information should be trustworthy and sourced from official providers.
- Display the last verification date for scholarship information.
- Separate scholarship programmes from their individual annual application cycles.
- Eligibility recommendations are guidance, not guarantees.
- Funding information must be transparent and specific.
- Students control their personal data.
- Consultancies and universities cannot automatically access all student profiles or documents.
- Students must consent before private information is shared.
- Institutions must be verified before receiving an approved institutional profile.
- Paid promotional placements must be labelled.
- Paid placement must not affect scholarship verification or eligibility recommendations.
- Do not make misleading claims about admission, scholarships, or success rates.
